Information We Collect

The bot may process WhatsApp phone numbers, group messages, uploaded files, and user-submitted content to provide its features.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide schedules, learning materials, task information, AI responses, and file archiving requested by users.

File Storage

Files submitted with the upload command may be stored in the administrator's Google Drive account. Files are not automatically archived without an explicit upload command.

AI Processing

Messages submitted to AI features may be processed by Google's AI services to generate responses.

Data Sharing

Information is not sold. Data may be processed by service providers required to operate the bot, including hosting, storage, and AI providers.

Data Retention

AI conversation history may be periodically deleted. Archived files may remain stored until removed by the administrator.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the bot administrator through the WhatsApp group where the bot is used.
